About ETHER VEENA

ETHER VEENA is a free, touchless spatial instrument that runs in your browser. Your camera follows your hands, and the space around you becomes the instrument: play a Rudra Veena, a harp or a theremin without touching anything.

Questions

What is ETHER VEENA?

ETHER VEENA is a touchless spatial instrument that runs in a web browser. Your camera tracks your hands, and where they are in the air decides what you hear. You can play a Rudra Veena, a harp or a theremin.

How do I play it?

Press Start and allow the camera. Hold both hands up in a comfortable position and press Calibrate. On the theremin, move your right hand left and right for pitch and your left hand up and down for volume. On the Rudra Veena the left hand slides along the neck and the right hand plucks. On the harp you sweep an open hand across the strings.

Is my camera video recorded or uploaded?

No. The hand and face tracking runs on your own device. Video is not recorded, saved or sent anywhere. The tracking models are downloaded from a public CDN when you first start the camera.

Which devices and browsers work?

Any recent desktop or phone browser with a camera and Web Audio, such as Chrome, Edge or Safari. On a phone the app keeps the screen awake while you play, and it can be installed to the home screen.

Is it free, and do I need to install anything?

It is free to use in the browser, with no account and no download. You can optionally install it as an app from your browser menu.

What else can it do?

It has ready-made presets for each instrument including Rudra Veena ragas by time of day, a Randomize function, a Polygon camera filter that hides your face, a Vector Camera that saves the polygon picture as SVG, PDF or PNG, and saved presets of your own.
